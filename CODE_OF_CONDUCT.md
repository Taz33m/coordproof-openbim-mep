# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in CoordProof a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique designs and evidence, not people.
- Welcome questions from contributors with different CAD/BIM backgrounds.
- Disclose uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy and do not publish confidential project data.

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct,
doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and
other contributions that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently
ban participants for conduct they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Report conduct privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile or the
repository's private reporting channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as practical. This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
